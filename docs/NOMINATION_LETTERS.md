# Nomination letters

BRADIX includes a TMR nomination draft template. The template must be personalised and verified before use. A nomination is not a guarantee of employment, appointment, or outcome.

## Mills draft structure

Use the recipient’s correct name, role, contact details, and consent. State the relationship to the nominee, the role or pathway being supported, and two or three specific, verifiable examples of capability. Avoid unsupported superlatives. Close with a clear contact method and permission for follow-up.

## Woodward draft structure

Use the same evidence standard, but write an independent letter rather than copying the Mills draft. Include the relevant experience, public-service qualities, and concrete examples that are actually known to the writer.

Before sending, check spelling, dates, job title, recipient, attachments, privacy consent, and whether the submission channel is official. Keep a copy of the final sent version and any acknowledgement in the evidence vault.
