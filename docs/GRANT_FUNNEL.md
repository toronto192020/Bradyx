# Grant funnel

BRADIX can help keep a grant search organised, but every opportunity must be checked against the issuing body’s current rules. Use the funnel to turn a broad scan into one verified next action.

| Stage | Required action | Output |
|---|---|---|
| Scan | Search official government, council, and foundation sources. | Candidate link and closing date. |
| Filter | Confirm applicant type, geography, purpose, co-contribution, and deadline. | Eligible / not eligible / unclear. |
| Evidence | Collect the guidelines, budget inputs, outcomes, and supporting records. | Dated evidence folder. |
| Draft | Prepare answers in the funder’s requested structure. | Reviewable draft. |
| Submit | Confirm authority, attachments, declarations, and receipt. | Submission receipt. |
| Report | Calendar milestones and retain acquittal records. | Reporting trail. |

Do not infer eligibility from a search result or automated summary. Save the official guidelines and re-check the closing date before submission.
