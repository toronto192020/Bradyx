# PTQ protection playbook

This playbook is a controlled process for requesting accounting support and preserving a defensible record. It is not accounting, tax, or legal advice.

Begin with a one-page fact sheet: entity or individual name, ABN or relevant identifier, period covered, issue, requested outcome, known deadlines, and documents available. Use BRADIX to draft the request, then have the responsible person or adviser check every figure and assertion.

## Request structure

1. State the matter and period in the first paragraph.
2. List the exact questions or deliverables required.
3. Attach a numbered evidence index rather than a loose bundle.
4. Ask for fees, scope, assumptions, and expected turnaround in writing.
5. Record the response and schedule the next review date.

Protect originals, do not alter source records, and keep a separate note of any corrections or missing data.
