# Maximum entitlement stack

This playbook is a preparation and record-keeping aid for Andrew’s conversations with Services Australia and other providers. It is not a determination of eligibility. Eligibility, rates, waiting periods, and reporting obligations must be confirmed through official channels.

## Prepare one evidence pack

Create a dated folder containing identity documents, residency evidence, income and asset information, caring-responsibility evidence, medical or functional reports where relevant, rent and utilities, existing payment letters, and a contact log. Keep originals unchanged and store copies in the BRADIX evidence vault.

## Conversation sequence

Start by asking which payment, supplement, concession, or carer-related pathway is applicable to the current circumstances. Ask the officer to record the caring relationship, the effective date, the evidence still required, and any review or appeal rights. Request written confirmation of decisions and deadlines.

## BRADIX checklist

| Item | Record |
|---|---|
| Agency and channel | Name, reference, date, time |
| Matter discussed | Plain-language summary |
| Evidence supplied | Filename and timestamp |
| Decision/deadline | Exact wording and due date |
| Next safe action | One reversible action |

Never submit a claim solely from an automatically generated draft. Confirm the correct official form, deadline, and supporting evidence first.
