# BRADIX Dashboard Design Brief

## Three Stylistic Approaches

### Signal Room
A dark operations room with electric telemetry, compact readouts, and urgent motion. Probability: 0.04.

### Clinical Glass Ledger
A calm glassmorphism command surface for care, admin, and evidence, with semantic status colors and generous legibility. Probability: 0.08.

### Paper Trail Console
A warm, archival interface inspired by indexed folders and public-service forms, made tactile through paper textures and tabbed navigation. Probability: 0.03.

## Chosen Approach: Clinical Glass Ledger

**Design movement:** Modern glassmorphism with clinical information design.

**Core principles:** Calm authority, legibility under pressure, semantic status color, and deliberate touch-first interaction.

**Color philosophy:** Ink-black surfaces create focus; ice-blue marks the active system; mint means safe and complete; amber means attention; coral means a blocked or critical path.

**Layout paradigm:** A persistent left rail anchors identity while the main canvas uses a staggered operations layout: live care timers first, then action queue, evidence, and situations.

**Signature elements:** A visible BRADIX pulse mark, frosted glass panels with hairline borders, and thin telemetry rails used as progress and status indicators.

**Interaction philosophy:** Taps should feel deliberate and reversible. Every action produces a clear result in the activity rail. Destructive or outbound actions are marked as drafts until a real connector is configured.

**Animation:** Use restrained 160–240ms ease-out transitions, pulse only for active timers or new activity, and honor reduced-motion preferences.

**Typography system:** Outfit for display and navigation; Inter for data, labels, and supporting copy. Large numbers use tabular figures.

**Brand essence:** A personal command surface that turns care, admin, and evidence into one calm next action. Personality: dependable, observant, direct.

**Brand voice:** Direct, supportive, and never vague. Example lines: “The next safe action is ready.” “Evidence captured locally; sync is optional.”

**Wordmark & logo:** A geometric B with a pulse cut through the counterform, used as a compact cyan mark rather than a text logo.

**Signature brand color:** Ice cyan #8DEBFF.

## Style Decisions

- Default to a dark, high-contrast surface with no purple gradients.
- Prefer staggered/asymmetric modules over a centered marketing layout.
- Keep outbound actions explicitly labelled as draft or connector-dependent.
- Keep local-only features fully usable without a backend.
